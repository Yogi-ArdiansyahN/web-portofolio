## Business Context
Provides health summaries, consultations, and nutrition guidance through the web and mobile apps with AI support. The system manages patient data sharing, subscriptions, and cross-device notifications.

## Role & System Flow
* Designed the health summary sharing mechanism with one-time or ongoing access modes, including data snapshots, approval time, and expiry in the API.
* Implemented the Web Push flow from retrieving the VAPID key and browser subscription to sending the subscription to the server and cancelling it locally and on the server.
* Maintained i18n key and test consistency when adding sharing and cross-client notification flows.
* I used AI-assisted coding to create components and tests, then reviewed access logic, approval time, and expiry manually.

## Technical Notes
I designed the sharing and notification flow across web, mobile, and backend, with one-time and ongoing access modes in the API. I created components and tests, then reviewed access logic, approval time, and expiry manually.
